# Get started with Research Monitor

Research Monitor reads new papers every night in three fields: gut biology, cancer cell therapy, and single-cell foundation models and perturbation prediction.

1. Show the person's reading list with the Research Monitor reading list tool. If it says they follow no field yet, they are new to Research Monitor.
2. If they are new, send them to research-monitor.com/onboarding first, in two or three sentences: signed in with the account they just connected, three quick checks set up their space from their own public record (who they are, their papers and lab, and what they are working on now), and it fills in while they look around. Say that they can come back here afterwards and ask about their field, and that they can also just pick a field here now instead.
3. When they come back, or if they would rather stay in the chat, ask which of the fields above is closest to their work. If their work spans several, ask which one matters most first.
4. Follow that field for them with the Research Monitor follow tool, using the field's slug.
5. Show their reading list: the newest papers in the field they now follow, with each paper's headline finding.
6. Tell them what they can do next, in one or two sentences: ask what's new in the field or what is moving in it, ask for the evidence on a question, see where papers in the field disagree, get a lab meeting brief for a paper, place a paper or idea on the field map, test draft claims against the evidence, or keep watching a claim.

If the follow tool answers PLAN_REQUIRED, the account's plan already follows as many fields as it includes; the answer names the field it follows, and fields are changed at research-monitor.com/topics. Carry on with the field they already follow.
